📋 My Job Description

Salesforce Administrator · Developer · Consultant — a reminder of everything this role actually covers.

I own WGU's Salesforce org end-to-end — administration, development, and internal consulting — supporting partner management and enrollment operations across the platform.
⚙️

Administration

The Admin Hat
  • Users, security, permissions, and org configuration
  • Declarative automation with Flow
  • Data quality, deduplication, and imports/exports
  • Org health & release readiness for seasonal Salesforce releases
🔧

Development

The Dev Hat
  • Custom solutions with Apex and platform APIs
  • Integrations — including the Salesforce → Tealium CDP integration
  • Experience Cloud sites — partner portals and external experiences
  • Sandbox strategy + deployments via GitHub Actions CI/CD
🤝

Consulting

The Consultant Hat
  • Lead discovery with stakeholders; translate requirements into technical designs
  • Advise on declarative-vs-code and build-vs-buy decisions
  • Process mapping and stakeholder alignment
  • Train end users; serve as the platform escalation point
