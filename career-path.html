Verdict

Four ideas that sum up the table.

The floorWGU

Stable pay at or above market, plus the Senior title.

The ceilingFDE or Solutions Engineer

They fit your mix of tech, AI and selling, and pay $200–350K+ without the CTA grind.

The freedom playThe LLC

Start small now and grow it into income you don't have to trade hours for.

Keep openGTM, Enterprise AE and the CTA

Take a GTM or AE role if an offer beats your pay and builds your skills. Treat the CTA as a long-term maybe, since your architect work counts toward it anyway.

Smart path

What to do, in order.

Years 0–2SLC, SE II → Senior
  • Get promoted to Senior at about $165–190K. Negotiate for $165K+, not the $144K floor.
  • Learn Python and build 2–3 real AI or agent projects connected to Salesforce.
  • Earn Platform Developer II and Agentforce Specialist.
  • Take one or two small consulting gigs through the LLC.
Years 2–4Make the jump
  • Apply for FDE or Solutions Engineer roles at AI or Salesforce-ecosystem companies.
  • Target $200–260K total.
  • Fallback: Staff at WGU, which is still about $200K.
Years 4–10Build freedom
  • Grow as a Senior FDE/SE at $260–350K+, or turn consulting into a small shop.
  • Invest aggressively. Savings rate is what gets you semi-retired in Scottsdale by 40, not salary alone.

Offer test

When an offer comes in, ask: does it…

  1. Pay more?
  2. Build skills?
  3. Move you toward FDE, Solutions Engineer or consulting?

2 out of 3 = look seriously.
