Verdict

  • WGU is the floor. It gives you stable pay at or above market, plus the Senior title.
  • FDE or Solutions Engineer is the ceiling. They fit your mix of tech, AI and selling, and pay $200–350K+ without the CTA grind.
  • The LLC is the freedom play. Start small now and grow it into income you don't have to trade hours for.
  • Keep GTM, Enterprise AE and the CTA open. Take a GTM or AE role if an offer beats your pay and builds your skills. Treat the CTA as a long-term maybe, since your architect work counts toward it anyway.

Smart Path

Years 0–2 (SLC, SE II → Senior)

  • Get promoted to Senior at about $165–190K. Negotiate for $165K+, not the $144K floor.
  • Learn Python and build 2–3 real AI or agent projects connected to Salesforce.
  • Earn Platform Developer II and Agentforce Specialist.
  • Take one or two small consulting gigs through the LLC.

Years 2–4 (make the jump)

  • Apply for FDE or Solutions Engineer roles at AI or Salesforce-ecosystem companies.
  • Target $200–260K total.
  • Fallback: Staff at WGU, which is still about $200K.

Years 4–10 (build freedom)

  • Grow as a Senior FDE/SE at $260–350K+, or turn consulting into a small shop.
  • Invest aggressively. Savings rate is what gets you semi-retired in Scottsdale by 40, not salary alone.

Offer Test

Does the offer pay more, build skills, or move you toward FDE, Solutions Engineer, or consulting? If it hits 2 of those 3, look seriously.
