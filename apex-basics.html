00

One object, one field

1 · Default the Rating

When an Account is created with no Rating, set it to Warm.

2 · Clean the partner code

Whenever an Account is saved — created or edited — its partner code should end up trimmed and uppercase.

3 · Stamp the email change

When a Contact's Email changes, set a date field to today. Editing any other field on the Contact must leave that date alone.

01

Reach to the parent

4 · Push the phone up

When a Contact's Phone is updated, copy it onto the parent Account's Phone.

5 · Flag the Account on Closed Won

When an Opportunity becomes Closed Won, mark its Account as a signed partner and record the date. Later edits to that Opportunity must not change the date.

Needs a checkbox and a date field on Account.

6 · Live Contact count

Keep a number field on Account equal to how many Contacts it has, as Contacts are added and deleted.

Needs a number field on Account.

02

Guardrails

7 · One primary Contact

An Account can only have one primary Contact. When someone marks a Contact primary, un-mark every other Contact on that Account. No error — just make it true.

Needs a checkbox on Contact.

8 · Block the close

An Opportunity cannot go Closed Won unless its Account has a primary Contact. Stop the save and tell the user why.

9 · Protect the Account

An Account with open Opportunities cannot be deleted.

03

The ones that bite

10 · Contact changes Account

Your count from drill 6 goes wrong when a Contact is moved from one Account to another. Make both Accounts end up correct.

11 · Cascade the deactivation

When an Account's status changes to Inactive, mark all its Contacts inactive too. Setting the Account back to Active must not reactivate them.

Needs a checkbox on Contact and a status field on Account.

12 · Stop the re-fire

Make your Contact handler update the same Contacts it was handed. Run it, read the error you get, then stop it happening without removing the update.
